# National projects

**Kind:** index
**Authority:** advisory — Post-1.0 direction; the charter mentions none of these
**Status:** draft
**Owner:** simulation
**Last verified:** 2026-08-28

A **National Project** is a temporary nationwide material and labour distortion: explicit
phases, reserved materials, project cargo, construction sites and priority rules that stress the
ordinary economy. It never lives in a separate tech tree, and the Space Programme is an
industrial and logistics project, not a flight simulator.

## The three projects

| Project | Historical basis | The mechanic it needs | Design page |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Space Programme** | Baikonur / Leninsk as a closed settlement; precision industry, electronics, chemicals, skilled labour, project cargo | multi-tier priority freight, an extended resource tree (electronics, precision components), a remote settlement, the [electronics cascade](../causal-loops.md#space-electronics-cascade--target) | [game modes — Space](../../product/game-modes.md) |
| **Housing Campaign** | 1957 pledge; Khrushchyovka standardisation; 84 million to house in twelve years | construction-material competition between housing and industry, a housing queue, mikrorayon completeness, quality-vs-quantity | [housing](../society/housing.md), [construction](../physical-economy/construction.md) |
| **Mobilization** | 1941: 1,360 factories evacuated in three months; rations tiered by labour category | factory conversion (civilian → military recipes at reduced efficiency), tiered rationing, industrial evacuation as physical logistics, priority inversion | [allocation](../planned-economy/allocation.md), [priorities](../planned-economy/priorities.md) |

## What every project shares

- **Priority relocates scarcity.** Copper to the Space Programme is copper not in radios; rail
  slots to project cargo are coal delivered late. The Planner must see the displaced use
  ([priorities](../planned-economy/priorities.md)).
- **The labour footprint is larger than the headcount.** Engineers pulled into a project bring
  households that need housing, food, heating, schools, clinics and transit
  ([workplaces](../society/workplaces.md)).
- **Storming amplifies.** A project behind schedule storms; its suppliers storm; the pulse
  propagates upstream and returns as late inputs ([storming](../planned-economy/storming.md)).
- **The privilege loop needs no penalty modifier.** What the project receives is exactly what
  other districts lose ([causal loops](../causal-loops.md#national-project-privilege--target)).
