## What belongs here

This section covers workplace and governance institutions that mediate between citizens and the
planned economy: enterprise trade-union committees, trade unions, local Soviets, and the
institutional forms of alternative socialist systems. Each institution is a filter between
physical reality and the Planner's knowledge — it gathers information, distorts it, and passes
it upward.

Production mechanics, resource allocation, and enterprise recipe behaviour do not belong here.
Those are economy-section concerns. The boundary: if a mechanism changes how information flows
from workers to the Planner, or how welfare is allocated within an enterprise, it belongs here.

All content in this section is Post-1.0 unless noted. The game's fixed 1950s–60s era
determines which institutional forms are period-accurate.
