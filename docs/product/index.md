What the game is, what 1.0 is, and what comes after. This section is the statement of intent;
mechanism lives in [simulation](../simulation/index.md), contract in
[specifications](../reference/specifications/README.md).

## Belongs here

The vision, the design laws, the player's role, the simulation philosophy, the 1.0 portal, the
Post-1.0 direction and the game-mode catalogue.

## Does not belong here

Mechanism detail (simulation tree), architecture (architecture handbook), code standards
(engineering), historical evidence (research).

5. [1.0 scope](scope-1.0.md) — the portal to the charter.
6. [Post-1.0](post-1.0.md) and [game modes](game-modes.md) — direction, not commitments.
